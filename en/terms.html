1. About the App

The App delivers one story recorded in the Buddhist texts each day, through a notification. In the English edition, the story text is taken from Bhikkhu Sujato's English translation of the Pali suttas (SuttaCentral, CC0) and is not rewritten in our own words. Longer stories are shortened, and each omission is marked with a note. Each story lists the source text it comes from and the translation used.

The stories, “Today's question,” and “One-minute practice” are offered as general information to help you reflect on daily life, and are not a substitute for professional advice. If you are struggling with your physical or mental health, please consult a doctor or another professional.

3. About the AI consult feature

“AI consult” is a feature in which, in response to a worry you write, an AI chooses one related story from the stories included in the App and shows how it connects to your situation in a short passage (the “bridge text”). In the English edition, the story is chosen only from the stories available in English.

  • The story text included in the App has been checked in advance and is never generated by AI. The AI does only two things: “choose one matching story from the catalog” and “write the bridge text.”
  • The bridge text is written by the AI each time and is different in nature from the story text, which has been checked by the developer. To the extent permitted by law, the developer does not guarantee the accuracy or appropriateness of the bridge text.
  • AI consults are not a substitute for medical care, psychotherapy, or religious guidance. If what you write is determined to suggest self-harm or suicide, the AI does not respond, and the App shows you where to find help instead.
  • You can use AI consults for free up to 3 times a month. The count resets on the first of each month, Japan time. To use more, you need to subscribe to Premium, described in Section 4.
